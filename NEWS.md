# MGWPR 0.0.1

* Initial public package release based on the uploaded MGWPR 0.6.2 source.
* Includes pooled and individual fixed-effects spatial panel estimators,
  simulation, coefficient mapping, inference, and spatial-panel diagnostics.
* Adds `mgwr()` as a cross-sectional MGWR interface to
  `GWmodel::gwr.multiscale()`, with per-term bandwidths and `sf` mapping.
